Hi, I'm Ryan Sun. I work on robot learning, with a focus on manipulation and embodied AI.

My interests include learning visuomotor policies from demonstrations, simulation-based evaluation of robot policies, and bridging the gap between simulation and real-world deployment.

Feel free to check out my [projects](/projects/) and [publications](/publications/), or reach out via the links below.
